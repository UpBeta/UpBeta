# Hi, I'm Fabrizio — Founder of UseNexus

**We build AI that answers the phone.**

UseNexus is a multi-channel AI voice platform that turns phone calls into intelligent conversations. We help businesses automate inbound and outbound communications with AI-powered voice agents.

## What UseNexus does

- **AI Receptionist** — Answers inbound calls 24/7 in 10+ languages
- **Outbound Campaigns** — AI-driven call, email & SMS campaigns with smart fallback
- **Appointment Confirmation** — Automated voice calls to confirm bookings
- **Lead Callbacks** — AI agents that call back leads with personalized briefings
- **Real-time Transcription & Sentiment Analysis**
